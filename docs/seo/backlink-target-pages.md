# Backlink target pages

Backlinks must be earned from relevant sites. This document is a prioritization guide, not evidence that any placement exists.

| Priority | Destination | Why it is link-worthy | Outreach / editorial angle | Natural anchor variations | Relevant source types |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `/` | Central entry point to ApplyCraft's multilingual resume, cover-letter and job-application workflow | Free multilingual career tools; privacy-first workflow | ApplyCraft, ApplyCraft.io, ApplyCraft resume tools | Career resource lists, startup directories, product communities |
| 2 | `/blog/teacher-resume-skills-achievements/` | Practical teacher skill-to-evidence table plus role-specific achievement examples | Resource for educators preparing applications | teacher resume skills, teaching skills and achievements, teacher resume guide | Teacher resource sites, education blogs, educator communities, career centers |
| 3 | `/blog/student-resume-summary-examples/` | Concise examples for a narrow student search intent | Student career-preparation resource | student resume summary examples, student profile examples | University/college career centers, student communities, internship resources |
| 4 | `/blog/student-resume-no-experience/` | Complete evidence-first resume guide for candidates without formal employment | First resume / employability resource | student resume with no experience, first student resume guide | Schools, youth employability programs, student resource pages |
| 5 | `/resume/templates/` | Browse 60 resume template models with multilingual support | Template resource for candidates comparing layouts | resume templates, ApplyCraft resume templates, multilingual resume templates | Career directories, design/resource lists, job-search guides |
| 6 | `/fr/blog/exemple-cv-maroc/` | Francophone Morocco-focused CV guidance connected to related French resources | Practical CV resource for candidates in Morocco | exemple CV Maroc, guide CV Maroc, CV au Maroc | Morocco career resources, francophone career blogs, student/employment organizations |
| 7 | `/fr/` | French entry point with unusually strong early Search Console visibility | Multilingual/francophone job-application tools | ApplyCraft en français, outils CV ApplyCraft | Francophone career sites, directories, education/employment communities |

## Rules

- Do not buy low-quality link packages.
- Do not automate spam outreach.
- Do not request misleading exact-match anchors.
- Prefer pages where the ApplyCraft resource genuinely complements existing content.
- Do not fabricate placements, endorsements, usage figures or partner relationships.
- Re-check the destination before outreach so the page is current, useful and indexable.
